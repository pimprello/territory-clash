# Channel text and art

Same layout as Army Clash's and Tower Clash's About pages: what the channel is, what happens in each Short, that it's a simulation, when new ones come out, the call to comment, the sister channels. (YouTube allows 1,000 characters; this is about 760.)

## Territory Clash (@TerritoryClashShorts, suggested handle)

Channel name: **Territory Clash**

Description:

```
Territory Clash: kingdom wars for the whole map, simulated and filmed in 4K.

Every Short is a brand-new 60-second war on the island of Vael. Two, three or four kingdoms start from their capitals; every territory grows troops, every order is a drag across the map, and armies that meet on the road cancel out. Capitals fall, pacts break, and whoever holds the most land at the bell rules the island.

Every battle is played out by the game's own rules and dice, with an original soundtrack made for every video. The kingdoms and their feuds are made up.

New wars every day at 10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 22:00 (UK time).
Pick your kingdom in the comments before the bell.

Sister channels: Army Clash and Tower Clash.
```

Add "Sister channel: Territory Clash." to the other two About texts when this channel goes live (their `CHANNEL.md` says how each is set).

## Art (`node brand.js` → `out/profile.png`, `out/banner.png`)

Made by the same rules as the other two channels' `brand.js`: a ray burst in the channel's colour, one shiny character in the middle of the profile picture, and six characters faded behind the channel name on the banner (same font, size and spacing).

- **Theme colour: emerald**, next to Army Clash's sky blue and Tower Clash's violet.
- **Profile picture (1024×1024):** a crowned king in amber, standing upright: the crown everyone is fighting for.
- **Banner (2560×1440):** six champions (Emberhold's pyro, Frostmere's frost witch, Verdance's druid, Duskveil's assassin, Ironreach's knight, Sunspire's paladin), faded behind TERRITORY CLASH; the name sits inside YouTube's safe area for every device.
